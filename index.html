A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before
we go.

Clear expectations make for a better trip. Still have a question? We’re a phone call away.

Talk to our concierge ↗
Getting a quote & reserving your date +

Send an inquiry with your date, passenger count, and plans. Our concierge will confirm availability and provide a personalized quote, along with the booking arrangements. An inquiry alone does not reserve the vehicle.

Changes & cancellations +

Please let us know promptly if your plans change. Our concierge will explain the applicable date-change and cancellation terms before you book, including any terms specific to holidays or major events.

Overnight & regional trips +

Day trips and overnight journeys beyond Nashville are available by arrangement. Tell us your destination and schedule, and we’ll include the route, chauffeur accommodations, and any special arrangements in your personal quote.

Airport transfers & extra stops +

We arrange private BNA arrivals and departures, point-to-point transfers, and itineraries with multiple stops. Share your flight information, luggage needs, and intended stops. We’ll confirm the pickup and waiting arrangements with you.

Payment arrangements +

Your concierge will confirm the price and payment arrangements directly. Payment is through the QuickBooks invoice we send, using the options on that invoice, or by Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, or Apple Pay.

Your group & the journey +

The Sprinter accommodates up to 9 guests, with 8 riding comfortably. The vehicle is private to your group. Share your passenger count, luggage, pickup details, and any particular needs so we can confirm the right arrangements before booking.
